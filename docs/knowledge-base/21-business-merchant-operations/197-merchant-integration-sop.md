# Merchant Integration SOP

**Status:** STABLE — there is no merchant-facing integration to operate an SOP around. See [`../17-merchant-documentation/76-merchant-integration-guide.md`](../17-merchant-documentation/76-merchant-integration-guide.md) for the customer/merchant-facing answer to "how do we integrate."

## What this SOP would cover if it existed, and what covers the equivalent work instead

A traditional "merchant integration SOP" would walk an internal team through onboarding a merchant's API/webhook integration. SahulatKar's equivalent internal work — making a *new, previously unsupported* domain extractable and checkout-automatable — is an engineering task, not a business-operations SOP, and is covered by the extraction-waterfall design ([`../05-architecture/microservices/product-service.md`](../05-architecture/microservices/product-service.md)) and the merchant-side friction-handling SOP ([`../12-operations/40-merchant-vendor-support-sop.md`](../12-operations/40-merchant-vendor-support-sop.md)) rather than a distinct business-operations process.
