## Latest Interventions

To strengthen the verification ecosystem, NADRA extended its expertise for convenient, secure and cost-effective Identity Verification services to the public and private sector. It is essentially a trust-building tool for the citizens. NADRA offers verification services including Biometric Verisys, SMS, Single Sign-on (SSO) through PakID App and Batch verification mode.

Revised definition of Biometrics with inclusion of Facial Recognition and IRIS

Launched Multi Biometric Verification System (MBVS) – Facial Recognition feature incorporated

Optimised customer onboarding framework through Nishan Pakistan Portal

Revision of agreements incorporating Information Security Protocols

Incorporation of Two-factor authentication for users accessing ID verification services

Revamped APIs to ensure data privacy

## Identity Verification Service (IDVS)

Integrated pathways for secure and seamless identity verification.

![Image 1: Corporate & Institutional Services](https://www.nadra.gov.pk/assests/verification/Identity_Pathways.svg)

### Biometric Verification

Biometric Verification (BIOSYS) is a streamlined ID verification service that matches fingerprints and a photograph with CNIC, providing instantaneous verification results electronically.

![Image 2: Corporate & Institutional Services](https://www.nadra.gov.pk/assests/verification/Biometric%20Verification.svg)

### Verisys

VERISYS is a web portal for citizen's consent (OTP)-based verification service that enables clients to instantly verify CNIC, NICOP and POC number through a secure medium.

![Image 3: Corporate & Institutional Services](https://www.nadra.gov.pk/assests/verification/Verisys.svg)

### Biometrically Enabled Batch Verification Service (API BASED)

BVS helps organizations handle large verification backlogs by enabling bulk biometric verification, ensuring accuracy and compliance while reducing delays.

![Image 4: Corporate & Institutional Services](https://www.nadra.gov.pk/assests/verification/Batch%20Verification%20Service.svg)

### Verification through e-Sahulat

Biometric Verification services are extended via the e-Sahulat franchise network, enabling citizens to verify identities conveniently across Pakistan.

![Image 5: Corporate & Institutional Services](https://www.nadra.gov.pk/assests/verification/Verification%20through%20e-Sahulat.svg)
