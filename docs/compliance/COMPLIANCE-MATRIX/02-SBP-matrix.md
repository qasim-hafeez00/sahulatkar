# SBP — Compliance Matrix

Covers: `docs/compliance/02-SBP/` (36 documents). See `00-MASTER-INDEX.md` for the Track A/B/C applicability legend.

**How SBP connects to SahulatKar:** SahulatKar itself is almost certainly not an SBP-regulated entity directly — that's an SECP NBFC matter. SBP's rules matter through the **partner institutions** SahulatKar must integrate with for money movement, identity/credit data, and payments (banks, EMIs, PSOs/PSPs, credit bureaus). Most rows below are tagged **"Applicable (vendor-contract requirement)"** — i.e. SahulatKar's contract with its payment/EMI partner must require the partner to hold/maintain the cited SBP status or control — rather than direct self-compliance. Rows tagged **"Applicable (self-imposed baseline standard)"** are not SBP-enforceable against SahulatKar directly but are adopted anyway as good practice given the sensitivity of the data/funds involved. Islamic-finance guidance is the one area that's genuinely direct-applicability regardless of licensing status, since it shapes product structuring itself.
