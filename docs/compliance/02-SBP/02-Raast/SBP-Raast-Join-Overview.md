## For Individuals

If you are an individual looking to register to using Raast services, please contact your account or wallet holding institution through its mobile app, internet banking, or branch.

## For Businesses/Merchants

Merchants and businesses can accept Raast payments by enabling Raast services through their respective banks or payment service providers. contact your bank or payment provider to integrate Raast for accepting digital payments.

## For Fintechs

Banks, EMIs, payment service providers, and other fintechs looking to integrate with Raast should reach out to the State Bank of Pakistan for onboarding and technical integration.
